How Serve works

You do the first step. Serve does the rest.

01

Tell Serve what your home needs

Give us your address and we pull up what we can about your home, so you confirm the details instead of filling them in. A real price and a real time slot in about a minute — no calling around, no waiting on three quotes to come back, no commitment.

02

Serve handles the logistics

We assign for the work you booked, confirm your window, pass on your access notes and parking, and stay on it through the visit. No vendor hunting, no quote chasing, no repeating yourself.

The right professional

Serve works with trusted Greater Boston service businesses and picks the one that fits the job and your home. You never have to vet anyone.

03

Everything stays in one place

Booking, scheduling, access, notes, follow-up — it all goes through Serve. And what we learn about your home, the pets, the parking, the room you asked us to skip, stays attached to it, so the next visit starts where the last one left off.

After you book

No mystery, no scramble. Here's exactly how the day runs — from the moment you reserve to the moment we lock up.

Within a few hours

We confirm your window

A real person calls or texts to lock in your arrival window and answer anything you left in the notes box. No automated runaround.

The day before

A reminder, and that's it

You get a text with your window and your cleaner's name. There's nothing to prep — clearing counters helps, but it's never required.

Getting in

Access and parking

Home, lockbox, doorman, or a hidden key — tell us which when you book. Flag tight parking or a permit zone and we plan around it.

Pets

They can stay right where they are

Mention your pets in the notes box. We keep doors closed, work around them, and use products that are safe once dry.

On the day

We bring everything

Supplies, vacuums, and equipment come with us — you provide nothing. Stay home or go about your day, whichever you prefer.

Before we leave

Photo-verified, then done

Every job ends with a walkthrough and photos. If something isn't right, tell us within 24 hours and we come back and fix it.
