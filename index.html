After you book

No mystery, no scramble. Here's exactly how the day runs — from the moment you reserve to the moment we lock up.

Within a few hours

We confirm your window

A real person calls or texts to lock in your arrival window and answer anything you left in the notes box. No automated runaround.

The day before

A reminder, and that's it

You get a text with your window and your cleaner's name. There's nothing to prep — clearing counters helps, but it's never required.

Getting in

Access and parking

Home, lockbox, doorman, or a hidden key — tell us which when you book. Flag tight parking or a permit zone and we plan around it.

Pets

They can stay right where they are

Mention your pets in the notes box. We keep doors closed, work around them, and use products that are safe once dry.

On the day

We bring everything

Supplies, vacuums, and equipment come with us — you provide nothing. Stay home or go about your day, whichever you prefer.

Before we leave

Photo-verified, then done

Every job ends with a walkthrough and photos. If something isn't right, tell us within 24 hours and we come back and fix it.
