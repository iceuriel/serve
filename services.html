Not included in any clean

Published deliberately. A scope people can read is what stops a job being renegotiated on the doorstep.

  • Exterior windows, or anything above ground-floor reach
  • Mold, biohazard, or hoarding-condition remediation
  • Post-construction debris
  • Pet waste and litter box contents
  • Furniture that takes more than one person to move
  • Laundry beyond linen changes

Run into one of these? Tell us in the notes box and we'll quote it separately.
